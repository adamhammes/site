## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 pound ground chicken
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 2–3 Thai chilies, finely chopped (adjust to heat tolerance)
- 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon fish sauce
- 1 teaspoon brown sugar
- 1/2 cup fresh Thai basil leaves
- 2 cups cooked jasmine rice

Optional (recommended for serving):

- 1–2 fried eggs

## Directions

1. Heat 2 tablespoons vegetable oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat.
2. Add minced garlic and chopped Thai chilies. Stir-fry briefly until fragrant (about 20–30 seconds), being careful not to burn.
3. Add the ground chicken. Cook, breaking it up into small pieces, until fully cooked and slightly browned.
4. Stir in oyster sauce, soy sauce, fish sauce, and brown sugar. Mix thoroughly and cook for another 1–2 minutes to allow flavors to combine.
5. Turn off heat and immediately stir in the Thai basil leaves, allowing them to wilt from residual heat.
6. Serve hot over jasmine rice. Top with a fried egg if desired.
