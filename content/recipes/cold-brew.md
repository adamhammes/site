## Ingredients

- 70g coffee, ground medium-coarse
- 3 cups cold water

## Directions

1. In a jar, stir together coffee and 1½ cups cold water.
   Cover and let rest at room temperature overnight or 12 hours.

2. Strain twice through a coffee filter, a fine-mesh sieve or a sieve lined with cheesecloth.
   In a tall glass filled with ice, mix equal parts coffee concentrate and water, or to taste.
   If desired, add milk.
